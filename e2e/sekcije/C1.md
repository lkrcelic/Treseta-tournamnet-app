# Section C1

**Area:** `e2e/tests/liga/c1-*.spec.ts` · **helper file:** `e2e/helpers/c1.ts` · **app id:** `c1`

The league night from start to finish, on phones: the flow where most of a night's bugs would show (in Bela, 1bb39ea: correcting a saved hand failed for a week; #74: a second tap on Završi meč).

Read `e2e/sekcije/README.md` first: where you work, how tests are written here, what "done" means and the report.

## Flows to cover
- **Admin, league "E2E · Novo kolo"** (`leagues.newRound`, 8 teams, no start time): Večer lige → pick the league → Novo kolo → Tko igra večeras → Kreiraj → Skica kola → Odobri sva → Objavi → Dnevni poredak shows the published round.
- **Players at a table of that round:** Tko igra (pick two players) → the scorepad: Upiši igru through the Zvanja step (3 and 4 per team) and the keypad (one team's points, the other gets the rest of 11) → Spremi; the hand is in the list with the right totals (points + zvanja).
- **Custom and "s ovim dosta":** a Custom hand (each team's points typed) that takes a team to 41: the "s ovim dosta" question comes; answering it for that team wins the match even with fewer points (the database's `dosta_side` and, after Završi meč, `Match.winner_side`). Use your own table or the open match of `tables.correction`, then put it back.
- **Correct a saved hand:** tap the hand → correct it step by step → change the points → Spremi; the list and the score show the corrected hand, and the database has one hand, not two. Also on `tables.correction` (league "E2E · Scorepad", first match with 4 hands).
- **Finish the match:** on `tables.finishMatch` (a team is at 41 and ahead) → Završi meč → a second, quick tap must not open a new hand entry (Bela #74) → Meč 2 → … → Kraj kola.
- **A tie at the target:** both teams level at 41 or more: the match plays on to 51 (the scorepad's "Do 51" flag), and only then is there a winner.
- **After the table closes:** Dnevni poredak and Ukupni poredak show the result, and the players' ratings moved.
- Treseta rules for the expected numbers: `src/app/_lib/game/scoring.ts` (`handError`, `zvanjaError`, `matchState`) and `BELA_SYNC.md` · Game rules.
