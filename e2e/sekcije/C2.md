# Section C2

**Area:** `e2e/tests/liga/c2-*.spec.ts` · **helper file:** `e2e/helpers/c2.ts` · **app id:** `c2`

Two phones of one team at the same table send the same hand: the second gets the warning and the hand is saved once.

Read `e2e/sekcije/README.md` first: where you work, how tests are written here, what "done" means and the report.

## Flows to cover
- `tables.twoPhones` (current league tonight, first match in play with 2 hands): two browser contexts logged in as the two players of one team (`team1` in the catalog entry).
- Both open the scorepad with the same board, both enter the same next hand (11 points split, maybe a zvanje); the first saves, the second gets the "board changed" warning (`HandsChangedWarning`, its Croatian text) and the hand count in the database is +1, not +2.
- After the warning the second phone shows the hand from the first one (reload or its own refresh) and can go on.
- The same with a correction: one phone corrects a hand while the other enters a new one.
