# Section C3

**Area:** `e2e/tests/turniri/c3-*.spec.ts` · **helper file:** `e2e/helpers/c3.ts` · **app id:** `c3`

A tournament day from sign-up to the end, as player, organizer-free admin and guest.

Read `e2e/sekcije/README.md` first: where you work, how tests are written here, what "done" means and the report.

## Flows to cover
- **Sign-ups:** `tournaments.open` (sign up, leave), `tournaments.fullWaiting` (the waiting list and its order; a team leaving lets the first waiting one in), `tournaments.closed` and `tournaments.cancelled` (refused, with the right text), `tournaments.naProlaz` (Kraj partije "na prolaz" shown on the tournament's page; the player's team is signed up).
- **Arrival:** `tournaments.arrival` (Tu smo for the player's team, the admin's arrival screen, closing arrival: who stays inactive, who comes in from the waiting list).
- **Kolo 1 and no-shows:** `tournaments.ready` → publish Kolo 1; at a table nobody answers: shift the round's `published_at` in the database to 4, 6 and 10 minutes back and check, after a reload or `/api/cron/tick`: the question "Jesu li … za stolom?" at 4, "Ne, nisu došli" accepted from 6, the table skipped at 10, the no-show result 5 : 0 per match (`NO_SHOW_SCORE`) and the team's ban.
- **Admin repairs:** Vrati stol and Uredi ishod on the live tournament (`tournaments.live`).
- **Na prolaz at the table:** on a tournament of your own with Kraj partije "na prolaz": the players' Custom key is Kaput (11 : 0), no "s ovim dosta" question, and a tie at 41 shows "Izjednačeno · još jedna igra" and plays one more hand.
- **Kolo 2 (Swiss):** pairings follow the standings, a bye when the count is odd; then the standings and the end of the tournament (`tournaments.finished` shows what a finished one looks like).

## Notes
Create your own tournament through the admin's Novi turnir (or Prisma in a fixture) for the flows that change state, so `--repeat-each=5` always starts from the same point.
