# Section C4

**Area:** `e2e/tests/liga/c4-*.spec.ts` · **helper file:** `e2e/helpers/c4.ts` · **app id:** `c4`

Vrijeme partije: when a match's time runs out, the match closes with the score it has.

Read `e2e/sekcije/README.md` first: where you work, how tests are written here, what "done" means and the report.

## Flows to cover
- `tables.timeLimit` (league "E2E · Scorepad"): a match in play with little time left.
- Shift `ends_at` into the past in the database, then reload the scorepad (and call `/api/cron/tick`): the match is closed at once with the entered score, the winner is the team ahead (`Match.time_expired`, `winner_side`), and the table goes on to the next match or the end. Vrijeme partije is 45 minutes here, 5 more for each raised target.
- A hand entered after the time is up is refused; with the score level, one more hand is played and decides the match (`service/ongoingResult/create.ts`, `TIME_UP`).
- The countdown on the scorepad shows the remaining time that the database says.
