# Section C5

**Area:** `e2e/tests/liga/c5-*.spec.ts` · **helper file:** `e2e/helpers/c5.ts` · **app id:** `c5`

The admin corrects a finished match: the match goes back into play, or a warning says why it can't.

Read `e2e/sekcije/README.md` first: where you work, how tests are written here, what "done" means and the report.

## Flows to cover
- `tables.adminEdit` (league "E2E · Scorepad"): a finished match the admin edits (change a hand, delete a hand, add a hand).
- If after the edit the hands give no winner (nobody at 41 and ahead, or a "s ovim dosta" call that no longer stands) and the table is from tonight with nothing after it: the match is open again at its table with the time it had left, and standings and ratings don't count it until it closes (Treseta 467ea599).
- Otherwise: the match stays without a winner ("Meč ostaje bez pobjednika?" asked first) and the warning says why it can't reopen.
- A match closed by Vrijeme partije goes to the team with more points after the edit; a level score leaves it without a winner, asked first.
- Otključaj partiju on a tournament table is the manual way, Bela's, with the sync of 17 Nov 2026: its test is a port of Bela's `tests/liga/c5-otkljucaj.spec.ts` (Treseta's hands, 45 minutes).
