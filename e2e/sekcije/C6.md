# Section C6

**Area:** `e2e/tests/prijava/c6-*.spec.ts` · **helper file:** `e2e/helpers/c6.ts` · **app id:** `c6`

Login, lockout, logout, sign-up and the first steps of a new account.

Read `e2e/sekcije/README.md` first: where you work, how tests are written here, what "done" means and the report.

## Flows to cover
- Login with username and with email, any case; a wrong password shows "Korisničko ime ili lozinka su netočni." (/api/login answers "Pogrešno korisničko ime ili lozinka.")
- Lockout after 5 wrong attempts in 5 minutes: use `accounts.locked` (`zakljucan`, kept for this) and check the right password is refused while locked.
- `accounts.inactive` (`deaktiviran`) can't log in, with its text.
- Logout (Odjava) ends the session: a protected page sends you to /login.
- Sign-up (/signup): validation errors in Croatian, then a new account with long Croatian names; the onboarding (`accounts.onboarding`, `novi.igrac`: birth date and "Pušiš li?").
- The Google button is visible; never click it.

## Notes
Lockout state lives in the server's memory: `app.sh stop` + `start` resets it, and the database lock needs more than 20 failures in 15 minutes, so a repeat-each run must use fresh usernames or wait it out: plan for it.
